### CHEM-000107: Practical density functional satisfying the full flat-plane condition
- **Subfield:** Theoretical and computational chemistry
- **Open:** Build an affordable functional that simultaneously respects piecewise linearity in electron number and constancy for fractional spin while giving reliable energies, densities and forces across bond breaking. Avoid case-specific corrections and artificial symmetry breaking.
- **Status:** Fractional-charge and fractional-spin failures were unified in 2008–2009. Fractional-spin corrections demonstrated successful molecular examples in 2018; a broadly transferable solution remains open.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** One approximation could describe charge localization and static correlation consistently.
- **Key works:** A. J. Cohen, P. Mori-Sánchez and W. Yang (2008), *Insights into current limitations of density functional theory*, [DOI:10.1126/science.1158722](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1158722); N. Q. Su, C. Li and W. Yang (2018), *Describing strong correlation with fractional-spin correction in density functional theory*, [article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6166809/).
- **Aliases:** Fractional-spin error; flat-plane problem.
